  that moved the position error by more than the seed noise.

  8.7-9.5 mm, still falling at the end).

  gamma, rollout length, KL). It is a property of the reward / geometry (the side-push optimum of the TODO M5
  watch item), not of the optimiser. Position error has been 7.3-9.9 mm in every config since run 07.
