## Decisions
- Damping is expressed as one time constant `damping_time` τ [s] in `stem.yaml` (stiffness-proportional: bend τ·E·I, stretch τ·E·A, twist τ·G·J), computed in `stem_cfg()`. Scales with the diameter like the stiffness; damping ratio of a mode is ζ = τ·ω/2. Value stays null until the stem stands in `check_stem.py` (τ ≈ 3.2 ms would give ζ = 0.05 for the first bending mode).
- `twist_modulus: null` for now: Newton then sets twist stiffness = bend stiffness (GJ = EI, i.e. Poisson ratio 0).
- Floats with an exponent in the yaml need a signed exponent (`5.0e+8`): PyYAML reads `5.0e8` as a string.
- Axial strain is measured as the gap at the joint along the tangent of segment i, not as the centre distance (which reports about -1.25e-3 false compression at the curvature limit, vs. 4e-5 real strain per 1 N).
- Stretch damage limit is a tensile stress (`damage.max_tensile_stress` [Pa], replaces `max_axial_strain`): stress = E · strain, tension only. A material property, so it stays fixed under stiffness/diameter randomization. Enforced in stage 1 once a threshold is chosen (changes yesterday's "bending limit only").
- `point_pose` offset is measured from the segment centre along the tangent [m].
