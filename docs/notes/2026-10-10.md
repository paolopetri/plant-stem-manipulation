- M5 plan (user, 2026-10-09/10): setup together on `feat/m5-training-setup`, then an unattended weekend loop on

  at launch), one change per run. Agents may change PPO values, existing reward weights / stds and fix proven bugs

  `tests/test_push_agent_cfg.py` in the same commit, marked `# exp/m5-weekend run NN`; no other test expectation and
  no `check_*.py` criterion may change (user).
- PPO starting values from Isaac Lab's `franka_soft` agent (MLP 256-128-64, obs normalization, init std 1.0, lr 1e-3
  adaptive, gamma 0.98, ...); `clip_actions` None (the action term clips each axis to [-1, 1]). 4096 envs.
- Videos: not during training (`--video` doubles the iteration time, 5.3 -> 10.5 s), but a 2-episode play video of
  the last checkpoint after every run, uploaded to the run's wandb page. Camera on env 0, robot and stem area.
- wandb project `stem-manip` is public (user), entity `ppetri-eth-z-rich`.
- Fine distance term as an ellipsoid (user): the height error scaled by 10/3 (std 1 cm sideways, 3 mm in height =

  54 % of the fine term (now 7 %). `height` term stays at weight 0. Targets stay reachable by construction (between
  the bowl and the deepest shape within 0.8 x the curvature limit).
