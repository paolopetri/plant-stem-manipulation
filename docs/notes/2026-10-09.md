## Decisions
Decided with the user on 2026-10-08 (M4 step 1, commands and stem observations); recorded here with the implementation:
- Stem point: the tip proper (segment 19, offset +L/2, arc length 0.40 m), task cfg.
- Target: once per episode, horizontal distance 3-10 cm from the tip's rest position, direction over the full circle; height on the "bowl" the pushed tip moves on (rest height - 3 r^2 / (5 s), axial shortening of a cantilever with a tip load, small deflection) +- 1 cm (revised below). At 10 cm the small-deflection chain needs 0.5 N at the tip and bends the lowest joint to 1.9 1/m (limit 5 1/m).
- Stem observation: stem base + 5 points equally spaced along the arc length (0.08 ... 0.40 m), robot base frame, raw meters (empirical normalization at M5); the 5 points also from the previous policy step (Isaac Lab observation history, oldest first) instead of a velocity: a camera delivers the same two frames. No separate stem-point term (the tip is the last point). Target observed as its position (robot base frame).
- Flagged assumptions: target fixed in the episode; upright stem and robot base for the target's rest position; full circle of directions, including towards the robot (front half = one cfg change); points on the centre line, positions only, no noise or latency yet.
- Target height revised after the code review (user): the +-1 cm band put 45.6 % of the targets above the reachable height (exact geometry with the curvature limit: at most 0.2-1.7 mm above the bowl for r 3-10 cm, and only with the base bent to the limit; my earlier claim that pushes at other heights also reach below the bowl was wrong). New: drop uniform between the bowl (c = 0.6 in drop = c r^2/s) and the deepest shape whose peak curvature stays within 0.8 x `damage.max_curvature` (4 1/m), so that the policy has to use the fork's slot (C / S bends need a moment at the tip; a prong's side alone gives the bowl). Shape model: force + moment at the tip, w = a x^2 + b x^3, c(t) = t^2/15 - t/10 + 0.6, peak curvature max(|6 - 2t|, |4t - 6|) r/s^2 (t = tip slope in r/s); deepest c 0.93 at 10 cm (8.3 mm below the bowl), 3.03 at 3 cm (5.5 mm). The shapes depend on length and curvature limit, not on stiffness (stiffness sets the force).
- Stem spawn area changed to x 0.50-0.65 m, y +-0.15 m (user, 2026-10-09; replaces x 0.275-0.525 m of 2026-10-06): near edge from joint 4's limit (a target up to 10 cm towards the robot puts the tool tip at about x 0.40 m at the stem-tip height, margin about 0.36 rad), far edge from the lab's plate (rods can only stand on the 58 x 58 cm plate, x <= 0.655 m). Shrinking the box towards the robot side alone did not help: the problem is the near side. A more refined strategy (radial region, base-dependent target directions, other fork orientations, joint-margin termination) after one more investigation: TODO "After the first training".
- M4 step 3, rewards and terminations (user, this session). Rewards (weights a first guess for the baseline):
  distance stem tip to target `1 - tanh(d / std)`, coarse 5 cm (+1) and fine 1 cm (+1); height error alone
  `1 - tanh(|dz| / 3 mm)` with weight 0 (ready for the side-push optimum, see below; one term, no coarse / fine pair:
  height errors span only ~0-23 mm, the side-push gap 0-8.3 mm lies on the 3 mm slope, larger errors occur only
  before the push, where the 3-D coarse term gives the gradient); approach `1 - tanh(d / 0.1 m)` of the tool tip to
  the nearest point on the stem (+0.5; exploration: the task terms are flat until the fork touches the stem; does not
  say where to push); curvature `sum max(0, kappa - 4)^2` above 0.8 x 5 1/m (-1); contact force
  `max(0, F - 2 N)^2` (-1); `action_rate_l2` (-0.01); `is_terminated` (-10).
- Terminations: time out; curvature above 5 1/m at any joint; contact force above 5 N; any FR3 joint within 0.25 rad
  of its limit (termination only, no penalty). No tool-tip out-of-bounds termination (user: it would limit the motion
  too much; removed from the TODO).
- Contact force included already in the baseline (user): rewards and terminations are training-only, the policy
  infers the force from `target_offset` and the stem deflection. Force of the robot (fork + arm links, one sum) on
  the stem, normal + friction, mean over the policy step (sensor history = decimation, against single-step spikes).
  5 N is a placeholder `damage.max_contact_force` in `stem.yaml`. Changes M5: no separate "without contact" baseline;
  the second step keeps the privileged critic observation.
- Side-push local optimum (user question): a prong's side alone only reaches the bowl, 0-8.3 mm above the target,

  added to the command to detect it. Later idea (user): two targets in sequence (TODO "After the first training").
