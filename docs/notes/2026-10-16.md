# Grilling: slot use after `m6-first`

## Done
- `/grill-with-docs` on how the policy is brought to use the slot (ADR 0004, the `m6-first` log, #170's comment).
  Analysis added to `docs/experiments/2026-10-16-m6-first.md`: the policy stops on the bowl (final `height_error`
  follows the target depth), and with fork_v2 the region below the bowl is out of reach within the limits (slot moment
  12–15 N per contact at a 2 cm lever; pressing 6–12 N compression, buckling load 1.55 N).
- Glossary: **Fork contact** (as in the main checkout's uncommitted change), **Slot contact**, **Prong contact**,
  **Slot moment**.

## Decisions
- Slot use through incentives, not contact rules; fork redesign for the slot moment: `docs/adr/0005-slot-use-through-incentives.md`
  (user, 2026-10-16). ADR 0004's top-4-segment fallback is superseded; the criterion "median `contact_point` < 0.32 m"
  is dropped.
- For `/to-spec` (user, 2026-10-16):
  - Potential-based shaping into the slot replaces `approach` (not into the roll); a share of resets with the stem
    crossing the slot of a table start pose; randomized fork-stem friction.
  - Compression limited relative to the buckling load (pressing the tip is unwanted, some axial force is fine); the
    term stays off on `main` until its value is set.
  - Targets only where all active damage limits allow them; no lever passes f ≈ 0.6 within the curvature budget.
  - Metrics, logged in training (wandb) and in `evaluate_policy.py`: `slot_contact` (share of fork-contact steps),
    `time_to_slot`, `slot_at_reach`; only slot contact counts, not the stem between the prongs without contact. The
    evaluation also records `target_depth`, r, the start-pose index and compression.
  - `scripts/check_slot_moment.py` before the next run: a slot moment on one segment and across two (pair reading
    against per-contact-point forces), then the full sequence (stem through the slot, roll until both prongs touch,
    keep rolling). The contact-force reading is fixed after it, so that the two forces of a couple do not cancel.
  - The user sets the contact-force penalty threshold from the check's forces.
- Fork redesign: wide V opening ("pizza") with inward hooks at the prong ends, lever about the prong gap; sized from
  the stem's measured stiffness (cantilever test); the user takes it to the supervisors. Expected early next week.
  A hooked fork stays non-prehensile while the opening between the hooks is wider than the stem.
- Until then: experimental runs on fork_v2 on an unmerged `exp/slot-moment-fork-v2` branch, off `main` once the
  changes above are merged: contact-force termination off, penalty threshold 20 N, compression limit at 1 x the
  buckling load, the largest per-contact force logged. The stem's stiffness stays as it is.

## Open questions
- For the supervisors: the fork redesign, the contact-force limit and the compression limit's value (#82).
- The stem's bending stiffness: measure it on the test plant; every force above scales with it.
# Spec #207: slot use through incentives

## Done
- `/to-spec` from the grilling above: #207 (`ready-for-agent`). Test seams confirmed by the user: `test_stem_state`
  (one slot reading), `test_stem_target`, `test_policy_eval`, `test_push_task_cfg`, `check_push_terms`,
  `check_push_obs`, the new `check_slot_moment.py`, `record_rollout.py compare`.

## Decisions
- The potential into the slot (user, 2026-10-16, #207): it pulls only until the stem is in the **entry zone**, the
  slot's axis from the slot base to 2 cm inside the prong tips; nothing pulls toward the slot base. The distance is
  measured around the prongs (the shortest path in the fork's plane that doesn't cross the fork), so that a prong's
  outer side is no local optimum. Φ = −tanh(d / std), zero in the entry zone; std 0.1 m and weight 0.5, as
  `approach`.
- No interim runs on fork_v2 (user, 2026-10-16, #207): the next training run waits for the new fork. The
  `exp/slot-moment-fork-v2` branch and its settings above are dropped. `check_slot_moment.py` runs first with the new
  fork; the contact-force reading fix and the target bound's model wait for it. ADR 0005 updated.

## Open questions
- Before the next run (#207): the share of in-slot resets, the friction range and its source; after the check: the
  target bound's model, the contact-force reading's aggregation and `CONTACT_FREE_FORCE`.
