## Decisions
- Approach threshold: bent stem in the slot as the geometry model, 1 mm margin on d (user, 2026-10-14, #71).
- `check_stem` tolerances: frequency -5 % / +0.5 % around 5.109 Hz, damping ratio ±0.007; reference = exact chain without armature, tolerance = sum of the known differences (user, 2026-10-14, #70).
- Clamp exemption tolerances in `check_push_agents`: position offset ≤ 1 + 0.05 (speed cap × physics step / clamp offset = 10 cm/s × 2 ms / 4 mm); rotation offset ≤ 1 + 0.03 (45 °/s × 2 ms / 3°), i.e. "within one physics step of 1"; no rotation leg in the clamp agent (user, 2026-10-14, #69).
- Robot base at the env origin in `check_contact` / `sweep_impedance`: tol 1e-5 m / 1e-5 rad (user, 2026-10-14, #61).
- `ToolTipKinematics` in `mdp/`, the pure functions in `utils/` (`isaaclab.utils.math` counts as plain torch math there) (user, 2026-10-14, #61).
- Tool-tip check (#72): reference = FR3 datasheet DH, tol 1e-5 m / 1e-5 rad; torque mode = each joint moves > 0.01 rad under a ±0.5 N m pulse; "start pose held" replaced by "robot gravity off" (the real FR3 compensates gravity itself) (user, 2026-10-14).
- `/improve-codebase-architecture` is part of the cleanup and runs right after #47, not after the midterm (user, 2026-10-14, #123).
