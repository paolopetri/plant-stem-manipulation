# 2026-10-08

## Decisions
- Tests first (user): `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` and the project `CLAUDE.md` ("Checks first"): expected values of checks, sweeps and unit tests come from the decided values, are set before the code changes, and are never loosened to make a run pass without the user's decision.
- Target clamp 4 mm / 3 deg: 4 N at K_p 1000 (2 x the lag criterion, about 3 x the measured push forces 0.8-1.4 N); 3 deg = 5.2 N m at K_o 100, 43 % of the FR3 wrist torque limit 12 N m (10 deg would be 17 N m, above it).
- The clamp acts at every physics step (option D), not once per policy step: clamping the step's end target against the tool's current pose counted the whole next step as stretch and capped the free-space speed at max_target_offset - lag (the +y ramp stalled at 4.05 mm per step instead of 6.4 mm). Per physics step the clamp is inactive in free motion (the distance is the lag) and bounds the spring force exactly when blocked. The stored target is the clamped one at the end of the step; the applied step is the target's actual motion.
- While the clamp holds the target, the feedforward velocity is the tool's velocity (the held target moves with the tool), not 0 and not the step's: then the damping adds no force to the 4 N, and the controller does not chatter between holding and releasing (second review, #1). A smooth-motion reward was proposed instead; it cannot remove a switch inside the controller, so it is added to the rewards for gentle approaches only (the impact momentum is not bounded by the clamp).
- The per-physics-step logic (interpolation, clamp, feedforward) is one torch-only function, `stem_manip.utils.impedance.substep_command`: the reference for the C++ port, where it runs in the 1 kHz loop.
- Speed cap 10 cm/s (was 20) and acceleration limit 0.08 m/s^2 (was 0.1), rotation limits unchanged (45 deg/s, 20 deg/s^2). Reasons: (1) 20 cm/s needs about 42 cm to reach and leave at the acceleration limit, more than the stem area, and full-speed moves lagged up to 3.35 mm; (2) under constant acceleration the lag settles at Lambda a / K, which at 0.1 m/s^2 and Lambda up to 21.5 kg (far, high points) is 2.15 mm, measured in reversals that really reverse; the first sweep's short moves (20 steps, reversals that only braked) ended before the lag had settled. At 0.08 m/s^2: <= 1.72 mm expected, 1.68 mm measured (about 15 % margin). D (inertia feedforward, option B3) later in the project.
- Joint-limit rule (user): moves within 0.25 rad of a joint limit are reported but not counted in pass/fail (sweep: start points after settling in the default mode, the whole move in `--full_speed`; env check: every check's measurement window). There the arm, not the controller, limits the motion.
- A push check with the task's action (stem pushed with the impedance action, contact force <= 4 N, no chattering) is deferred to the TODO; `check_push_env.py` stays a controller check in free space and against the ground.
